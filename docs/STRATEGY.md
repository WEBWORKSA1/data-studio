# Data.Studio: Strategy & Research Brief

*Prepared November 2026 for the Data.Studio domain.*

## 1. The winning idea

**Data.Studio = a free, private, in-browser "studio" of data tools, plus open data, live data and learning content, funnelling into a high-ticket lead-generation desk for custom dashboards and analytics work.**

Why this beats the alternatives:

| Option | Traffic potential | RPM / revenue per visitor | Moat | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pure blog about data | Medium, slow | AdSense only | Weak (AI answers) | Rejected |
| Dataset marketplace | Low early | Commission | Needs supply | Later phase |
| Paid SaaS BI tool | Low | High, but needs dev team | Strong but slow | Rejected (capital) |
| Content about Google's product | High | AdSense | **Trademark/UDRP liability** | **Rejected** |
| **Free tools + content + lead gen (chosen)** | High (tool keywords have large, evergreen, global volume) | AdSense floor + B2B lead value | Utility + backlinks + automation | **Chosen** |

Tool keywords ("csv to json", "chart maker", "pivot table online", "a/b test calculator", "sample size calculator") are evergreen, global, and attract natural backlinks from courses and forums. Every tool user is someone who works with data, the exact audience for B2B analytics services.

## 2. Revenue model (illustrative assumptions, not forecasts)

| Stream | Mechanism | Example at 50k sessions/mo |
|---|---|---|
| AdSense | Auto ads on tools, guides, live data | ~120k pageviews × $5–12 page RPM ≈ **$600–$1,450/mo** |
| Lead gen | Multi-step brief → proposal (or resell leads to agencies) | 0.15% → 75 briefs; 20% qualified → 15; 15% close → 2 deals × $4k ≈ **$8k/mo gross** (or 15 × $75 resale ≈ $1.1k) |
| Sponsorship | Tool / challenge / newsletter sponsors | 1–2 sponsors × $300–$1,500 |
| Donations | Tiered pledges | Small, mostly a community signal |
| Domain/site sale | Top-bar inquiry link | Option value |

**Takeaway:** AdSense is the floor. Lead generation is the business. Optimise every page for the brief form.

## 4. Trademark position (important)

In April 2026 Google renamed Looker Studio back to **"Data Studio"**, so the domain now matches a live Google product name exactly. Risk controls built in:

1. The brand is always written **Data.Studio** (with the dot), used in the generic, descriptive sense of "a studio for data".
2. **No content targets Google's product.** There are no "Data Studio templates", tutorials or connectors, which would be evidence of bad faith in a UDRP complaint.
3. A prominent non-affiliation notice appears in the footer and on `disclaimer.html`, and in the FAQ.
4. No Google logos or trade dress. Nominative mentions only.

**Residual risk to know about:** the top-bar "interested in this domain" link, combined with an exact-match famous mark, is the kind of evidence UDRP panels cite as an offer to sell. The strongest defence is a genuine, well-used, generic-meaning website, which is what this build is. Keep it that way.
