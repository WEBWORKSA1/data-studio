FAQ

Before you send a brief

Do I need to share my data upfront?

No. The brief only needs a description. For the audit we may ask for a small, anonymised sample, and we can sign an NDA first.

Which tools do you work with?

Spreadsheets, SQL databases, common BI tools (Power BI, Tableau, Metabase, Superset and Google's reporting tools among them), Python and R, and the APIs of most SaaS products. We recommend tools based on what your team can maintain.

How is pricing set?

Projects are quoted at a fixed price based on agreed deliverables. Retainers are a fixed number of hours per month. You'll always see the number before any work starts.

Who will do the work?

Vetted analysts, analytics engineers and BI developers from the Data.Studio talent network, matched to your stack and industry.
