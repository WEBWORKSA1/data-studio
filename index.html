FAQ

Questions, answered

Are the Data.Studio tools really free?

Yes. Every tool is free with no account required. The site is funded by advertising, sponsors, supporter donations and our custom data services.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Files you open in the tools are read and processed by JavaScript inside your own browser tab. Nothing is sent to us. Close the tab and the data is gone.

How often does the live data update?

An automated job refreshes the feeds every six hours from public sources including the European Central Bank (via the Frankfurter API), the World Bank, arXiv, Hacker News and GitHub.

Can you build a custom dashboard for my business?

Yes. Send a brief on the Hire Data Experts page. We'll reply with questions or a scoped proposal, and the first 20-minute audit call is free.

Is Data.Studio affiliated with Google?

No. Data.Studio is an independent website. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Google LLC, and it is not the Google product of a similar name. See our trademark notice.
