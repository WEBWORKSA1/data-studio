A studio for everyone who works with data.

Most people who work with data aren't data scientists. They're marketers, founders, students, nurses, teachers and analysts who need a quick, trustworthy answer. Data.Studio exists for them.

What we do

Data.Studio is an independent website with three jobs. First, we offer free tools for everyday data tasks: making charts, converting formats, profiling and cleaning files, building pivot tables and checking experiment statistics. Second, we help people find and understand data, through a curated directory of open data sources, a live data page that refreshes automatically, and plain-language guides. Third, we connect organisations that need more than a tool with specialists who can build it.

Our principles

Private by design

Our tools run entirely in your browser. When you open a file, it's read by JavaScript on your own device. It is never uploaded to us or anyone else. That's a technical design decision, not just a promise.

Honest numbers

Every calculator shows its method. Our guides explain both when a technique works and when it misleads. If we get something wrong, tell us and we'll correct it and note the change.

Free at the point of use

We keep the tools free with clearly separated advertising, sponsorships, supporter contributions and paid services. Sponsors and advertisers never influence tool results or editorial content.

How our live data works

An automated job runs every six hours. It requests fresh figures from public sources: the European Central Bank reference rates (via the open Frankfurter API), the World Bank's World Development Indicators, new papers on arXiv, community discussion on Hacker News, and trending open-source projects on GitHub. It saves the results as small data files that the site reads. Each feed shows when it was last refreshed, and each item links back to its original source.

Editorial policy

Guides are written to be practical and original. We cite sources where facts are not common knowledge, prefer primary sources, and date substantive updates. Links to third-party resources are chosen on merit. We do not accept payment for inclusion in the dataset directory or learning resources.
