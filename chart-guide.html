Three rules that beat any chart chooser

  1. One chart, one message. If you can't say the takeaway in a sentence, the chart is doing too much. Split it.
  2. Position beats colour, colour beats area. People compare lengths along a common axis most accurately. Angles, areas and colour intensity are harder to read, so use them for rough impressions.
  3. Label directly, title with the finding. Put labels next to the lines or bars they describe, and write titles that state the conclusion.
