Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

This policy explains what information Data.Studio ("we", "us") collects when you use data.studio (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Data you process in our tools stays on your device

Files and text you load into our tools (Chart Maker, CSV ⇄ JSON Converter, Data Profiler, Data Cleaner, Pivot Table and calculators) are processed by JavaScript in your browser. They are not uploaded to our servers or any third party. When you close the tab, that data is gone.

2. Information you give us

When you submit a form (contact, project brief, newsletter, pledge, contest entry, talent application or advertising inquiry) we receive the information you enter, such as your name, email address and message. Forms are delivered to us by email through FormSubmit, which processes the submission on our behalf. We use this information only to respond to you, provide what you requested, run contests and, if you subscribed, send our newsletter. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads that keep the Site free.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google My Ad Center, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent message from a Google-certified consent management platform before personalised ads are served. If you choose "Essential only" in our cookie notice, we request non-personalised ads.

4. Other technologies

  • Local storage: we store small preferences in your browser (your cookie choice and whether you dismissed a banner). These never leave your device.
  • Embedded videos: videos load from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com) only after you click play.
  • Fonts and libraries: we load web fonts from Google Fonts and open-source libraries from the jsDelivr CDN. These providers may receive your IP address as part of delivering the file.
  • Hosting: the Site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security and operations.

5. Retention

We keep form submissions as long as needed to handle your request and for a reasonable period afterwards (generally up to 24 months), unless the law requires otherwise. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act, or Canada's PIPEDA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, use our contact form and choose "Privacy request". You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Contests are open only to adults aged 18 and over.

8. International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours. Where required, they rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above and, for material changes, add a notice on the Site.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us through the contact form.
