How it works

Four steps to enter

  1. Pick a public dataset on the theme. Our dataset directory is a good start.
  2. Make one visualization. Any tool is fine: our Chart Maker, a spreadsheet, code or a BI tool.
  3. Publish it anywhere with a public link (your blog, a social post, a public gallery or a GitHub page).
  4. Submit the link with a 2–4 sentence story below before the deadline.

    Official rules (summary)

    • No purchase necessary. Entry is free. Payment or donation does not improve your chances.
    • Eligibility: open to individuals aged 18+ where skill contests are lawful. Void where prohibited. Data.Studio staff and judges may not win prizes.
    • Skill-based: winners are chosen solely on the published judging criteria. There is no random draw.
    • Entry period: from the 1st of the month to the last day of the month, 23:59 UTC. One entry per person per challenge.
    • Originality: entries must be your own work, use data you are allowed to use, and credit the data source.
    • Prizes: as listed on this page when the challenge opens. Prizes are non-transferable and winners are responsible for any taxes. If a listed prize becomes unavailable, a prize of equal or greater value may be substituted.
    • Licence: you keep ownership. You grant Data.Studio a non-exclusive licence to display your entry with credit on the site, newsletter and social channels.
    • Privacy: entry details are used only to run the challenge and contact you. See the Privacy Policy.
