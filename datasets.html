Before you use a dataset

  • Check the licence. CC BY needs attribution; ODbL needs share-alike for derived databases.
  • Read the methodology. Definitions change between releases and countries.
  • Note the vintage. Official statistics are revised. Record the download date.
  • Profile it first. Run it through our Data Profiler to spot gaps and outliers.
